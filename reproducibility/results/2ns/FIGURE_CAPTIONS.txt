Figure 1. Equilibrium structure and density. O–O RDFs at 295 K are above densities at 275, 295, 315 and 336 K; OFF is left and LES is right, following the archived paper layout. NPT runs are 1 ns, using the final 500 ps. Adapted error bars/bands are sample SD across seeds 17/29/43 after averaging three MD replicas within each seed; baseline uncertainty is MD-replica SD. Soper RDF reference is at 298 K. Broad coverage reduces absolute density error but makes both liquids underdense and overestimates the first RDF peak.

Figure 2. Transport from all 168 common 2 ns trajectories. Diffusion is above viscosity; OFF is left and LES is right. D_YH applies each trajectory’s Yeh–Hummer correction before seed averaging, retaining its covariance with raw D and viscosity. Both VACF D and Green–Kubo viscosity use the unchanged 5–10 ps running-integral mean at 0.5 fs correlation cadence. Adapted bars are three-training-seed sample SD; baseline bars are three-MD-replica sample SD. These are not confidence intervals. Finite-size-corrected D is an estimate; no larger-cell or viscosity-size extrapolation has been performed.

Figure A1. Molecular energy and Cartesian-force-component RMSE on parent-disjoint validation families at 123,200 optimizer updates. The original final-supplement builder and data are retained, with the original four-panel layout. Bars report mean and sample SD across training seeds 17/29/43. These are validation results, not the locked test set. Molecular results are unaffected by the MD stress correction.

Figure S1. Raw finite-cell VACF diffusion with viscosity, using exactly the main transport layout. Experimental diffusion is a bulk reference; raw values have not been finite-size corrected. The corrected comparison is Figure 2.

Figure S2. Signed normalized five-channel stress autocorrelation over 0–1 ps at all temperatures. No absolute value, smoothing, or positive-only logarithmic transformation is used. Bands are training-seed sample SD (baseline: MD SD). Each lane is normalized before aggregation.

Figure S3. Signed stress autocorrelation tails over 2–50 ps. Negative excursions and noise are retained. A tail close to zero alone does not establish convergence of its integral.

Figure S4. Running Green–Kubo viscosity through 50 ps, calculated from all native 0.5 fs stress records. Dark and light shading indicate 5–10 and 10–20 ps windows. Longer-lag sensitivity is retained; no window was selected to improve agreement with experiment.

Figure S5. Running viscosity through 20 ps with the same data and uncertainty conventions as Figure S4. Primary 5–10 ps scalar estimates reproduce the frozen completed analysis.

Figure S6. Normalized oxygen VACF from the full 2 ns trajectories. Each lane is normalized before averaging within training seed and then across seeds. Display thinning is separate from the full-cadence integration.

Figure S7. Raw finite-cell running VACF diffusion through 10 ps. The primary value is its 5–10 ps mean; the lane-wise finite-size shift is applied subsequently. No offset is added to this diagnostic curve.

Figure S8. Whole-window Green–Kubo estimates for disjoint 0–1 and 1–2 ns intervals. Thin lines connect the same fitted seed (baseline: same MD replica); small points expose all seed means. Nonoverlap is not proof of statistical independence. Bars retain the main figure’s sample-SD convention.

Figure S9. Disjoint-window Yeh–Hummer-corrected diffusion. The correction is recomputed using the viscosity and mean temperature of each same window; raw and corrected diffusion are therefore correlated with viscosity. Conventions follow Figure S8.

Figure S10. Mean viscosity from nonoverlapping 100, 200 and 500 ps blocks, compared with the full 2 ns estimate (horizontal dotted lines). Each block is independently demeaned and correlated; these means differ from the whole-trajectory estimator. Error bars retain seed/MD sample SD, not block SEM. Block-SEM columns are diagnostic only; independence and a confidence interval are not asserted.

Figure S11. Whole-prefix reanalyses at 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 ns for 295 K. Prefixes share observations and are correlated. These are not cumulative means of separate 100 ps estimates. The full all-temperature values and per-lane comparisons are supplied as CSV.
